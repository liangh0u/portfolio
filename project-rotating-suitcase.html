Rotating Suitcase

Staff / 参与者

ChaoWang, LiangHou

Rotating Suitcase is a multifunctional suitcase designed for long-distance travel and business trips, addressing the need for temporary seating and mobile work in airports and railway stations. Its innovative rotating top module can be quickly transformed into a temporary seat or work surface while retaining the essential functions of a suitcase. By integrating mobility, storage, seating, and workspace into one product, it provides a more flexible and convenient travel experience.

Rotating Suitcase 是一款面向长途旅行与商务出行场景的多功能行李箱，旨在缓解机场、车站候乘过程中临时休息与移动办公不便的问题。产品通过创新的顶部旋转结构，将行李箱局部模块快速转换为临时座椅或桌面，在保持行李收纳与移动功能的同时拓展使用场景，为旅途中等待、休息与办公提供更加灵活便捷的解决方案。
