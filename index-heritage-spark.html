Heritage Spark

Design Research

Wenqi Wu, Liang Hou, Yingping Cao, Xiaoran Zhang, Songruoyao Wu, Chongjun Zhong, Yang Yin, Zhibin Zhou, Kejun Zhang

We present Heritage Spark, a card-based design toolkit that supports novice designers in navigating the complex process of digitising intangible cultural heritage (ICH). Integrating design thinking with expert design methodologies, Heritage Spark helps teams connect cultural knowledge, stakeholder needs, and emerging technologies. Through three categories of cards and two thinking canvases, the toolkit guides novice designers from understanding the cultural context to framing problems, generating ideas, developing concepts, and iteratively refining their solutions.

我们提出了 Heritage Spark，一套帮助设计新手应对非物质文化遗产数字化复杂挑战的卡片式设计工具包。Heritage Spark 将设计思维流程与专业设计方法相结合，帮助团队在文化知识、利益相关者需求与新兴技术之间建立联系。通过三类设计卡片与两种思维画布，工具包引导设计新手从理解文化情境出发，逐步完成问题定义、创意生成、概念构建与方案迭代。

Research Question / 研究问题

Digitising intangible cultural heritage is a complex and multidisciplinary challenge. Novice designers often lack the cultural knowledge, technical understanding, and systematic methods needed to frame meaningful problems, leading them to converge prematurely on conventional or technology-driven solutions. This led us to ask: how can we develop an effective toolkit for digital ICH design, and how can a card-based toolkit support novice designers throughout this process? We explore how tangible design tools can scaffold problem framing, interdisciplinary collaboration, and creative exploration.

非物质文化遗产数字化是一项复杂的跨学科任务。设计新手往往缺乏相关的文化知识、技术认知与系统方法，难以识别和定义真正有价值的问题，容易过早收敛于常规或技术驱动的解决方案。因此，我们提出研究问题：如何构建一套有效的非遗数字化设计工具？卡片式工具又能如何支持设计新手的设计实践？我们探索实体设计工具如何为问题构建、跨学科协作与创意探索提供支架。

Design Process / 设计过程

We developed Heritage Spark through three steps. First, we translated the needs of problem framing into three card sets and two thinking canvases. Next, we embedded expert design practices into an accessible five-stage collaborative process. Finally, designers, technology experts, and ICH inheritors worked together to construct and refine the toolkit content, balancing professional knowledge, practical examples, and ease of use.

我们通过三个步骤完成 Heritage Spark 的设计。首先，从问题构建过程中的关键需求出发，推导出三类卡片与两种思维画布；随后，将专业设计方法嵌入易于理解的五阶段协作流程；最后，由设计、技术与非遗领域专家共同构建并校准内容，在专业知识、实际案例与使用门槛之间取得平衡。

Design Presentation / 设计呈现

Heritage Spark contains 40 cards across three categories and two thinking canvases. Together, they provide cultural knowledge, stakeholder perspectives, technological inspiration, and a shared workspace that guides teams from research to concept development.

Heritage Spark 由三类共 40 张卡牌与两张思维画布组成，为团队提供文化知识、利益相关者视角、技术启发与共享工作空间，引导设计者从前期研究逐步推进到概念形成。

Three Card Sets / 三类卡牌

As shown in Figure (a), the toolkit contains three card sets. ICH Cards provide cultural context, artistic features, and representative works to build foundational knowledge; Character Cards turn abstract users into concrete stakeholders with specific goals and tasks, supporting empathy and team collaboration; Technology Cards introduce emerging technologies through key insights, trade-offs, and application cases, helping teams overcome fixation and expand the solution space.

如图（a）所示，工具包包含三类卡牌。非遗卡牌提供文化背景、艺术特征与代表作品，帮助团队建立基础认知；角色卡牌将抽象用户转化为具有明确目标和任务的利益相关者，支持团队共情与协作分工；技术卡牌通过技术简介、优劣分析与应用案例呈现新兴技术，帮助团队突破思维定式并拓展方案空间。

Thinking Canvases / 思维画布

As shown in Figures (b) and (c), the toolkit includes two thinking canvases. The Individual Thinking Canvas supports independent research by recording cultural insights and stakeholder challenges before team discussion. The Team Thinking Canvas provides shared zones for pain points, ideation, solution summaries, and card placement, guiding teams from analysis and divergent thinking to concept synthesis.

如图（b）和图（c）所示，工具包包含两张思维画布。个人思维画布用于独立研究阶段，记录文化洞察与利益相关者问题，为团队讨论做好准备；团队思维画布设置痛点总结、创意构思、方案总结与卡牌放置区域，引导团队从问题分析和发散思考逐步走向方案整合。

Evaluation / 设计评估

We evaluated Heritage Spark in a 16-week design course with 64 novice designers divided into 21 groups. Using a sequential within-subjects design, each group first completed a digital ICH design task without the toolkit, followed by a 15-day washout period and a second task using Heritage Spark. The evaluation combined the USE questionnaire, creativity assessments of the submitted concepts by five design experts, and semi-structured interviews with student representatives to examine both the toolkit’s effectiveness and how it supported the design process.

我们在一门为期 16 周的设计课程中对 Heritage Spark 进行评估，共有 64 名设计新手组成 21 个小组参与。研究采用顺序被试内设计：各小组首先在不使用工具包的条件下完成非遗数字化设计任务，经过 15 天的间隔期后，再使用 Heritage Spark 完成第二项任务。评估结合 USE 问卷、五位设计专家对方案的创意评价，以及对学生代表的半结构化访谈，从定量与定性两个层面考察工具包的有效性及其对设计过程的支持作用。

Outcome / 研究结果

Findings / 研究发现

Compared with the baseline condition, concepts developed with Heritage Spark achieved significantly higher scores in novelty, feasibility, and completeness. The toolkit helped novice designers immerse themselves in stakeholder perspectives, broaden their technological knowledge, generate ideas from multiple dimensions, and establish clearer roles and shared understanding during collaboration.

与基线条件相比，使用 Heritage Spark 完成的方案在新颖性、可行性与完整性方面均得到显著提升。工具包帮助设计新手进入不同利益相关者的视角、拓展技术知识、从多个维度激发创意，并在团队协作中形成更清晰的分工与共同理解。
