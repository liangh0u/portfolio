Heritage Spark

Design Research

Wenqi Wu, Liang Hou, Yingping Cao, Xiaoran Zhang, Songruoyao Wu, Chongjun Zhong, Yang Yin, Zhibin Zhou, Kejun Zhang

We present Heritage Spark, a card-based design toolkit that supports novice designers in navigating the complex process of digitising intangible cultural heritage (ICH). Integrating design thinking with expert design methodologies, Heritage Spark helps teams connect cultural knowledge, stakeholder needs, and emerging technologies. Through three categories of cards and two thinking canvases, the toolkit guides novice designers from understanding the cultural context to framing problems, generating ideas, developing concepts, and iteratively refining their solutions.

我们提出了 Heritage Spark，一套帮助设计新手应对非物质文化遗产数字化复杂挑战的卡片式设计工具包。Heritage Spark 将设计思维流程与专业设计方法相结合，帮助团队在文化知识、利益相关者需求与新兴技术之间建立联系。通过三类设计卡片与两种思维画布，工具包引导设计新手从理解文化情境出发，逐步完成问题定义、创意生成、概念构建与方案迭代。

Background / 背景

Digitising intangible cultural heritage is a complex and multidisciplinary challenge. Novice designers often lack the cultural knowledge, technical understanding, and systematic methods needed to frame meaningful problems, leading them to converge prematurely on conventional or technology-driven solutions.

非物质文化遗产数字化是一项复杂的跨学科任务。设计新手往往缺乏相关的文化知识、技术认知与系统方法，难以识别和定义真正有价值的问题，容易过早收敛于常规或技术驱动的解决方案。

Image Placeholder

Research Question / 研究问题

How can we develop an effective toolkit for digital ICH design, and how can a card-based toolkit support novice designers throughout this process? We explore how tangible design tools can scaffold problem framing, interdisciplinary collaboration, and creative exploration.

如何构建一套有效的非遗数字化设计工具？卡片式工具又能如何支持设计新手的设计实践？我们探索实体设计工具如何为问题构建、跨学科协作与创意探索提供支架。

Image Placeholder

Design Framework / 设计框架

Heritage Spark enriches the five-stage design thinking process—Empathise → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test and Iterate—with expert practices such as problem framing, reflective practice, and the co-evolution of problems and solutions. It transforms an abstract methodology into a tangible and collaborative workflow for novices.

Heritage Spark 以设计思维的五个阶段——共情 → 定义 → 构思 → 原型 → 测试与迭代——为基础，融入问题构建、反思性实践以及问题与方案协同演化等专业设计方法，将抽象的方法论转化为适合设计新手使用的实体化协作流程。

Image Placeholder

Design / 设计

Heritage Spark consists of 40 cards across three categories—ICH Cards, Character Cards, and Technology Cards—along with an Individual Thinking Canvas and a Team Thinking Canvas. Together, they help designers understand cultural contexts, empathise with stakeholders, explore technological possibilities, organise insights, and transform them into grounded design concepts.

Heritage Spark 包含 40 张三类设计卡片——非遗卡片、角色卡片与技术卡片，以及个人思维画布和团队思维画布。它们共同帮助设计者理解文化背景、共情利益相关者、探索技术可能性、组织研究洞察，并将其转化为具有文化依据和现实价值的设计概念。

Image Placeholder

Evaluation / 设计评估

We evaluated Heritage Spark through a 16-week design course involving 64 novice designers. Participants completed digital ICH design tasks both with and without the toolkit, while five design experts assessed their proposals. Results showed that designs created with Heritage Spark achieved significantly higher scores in novelty, feasibility, and completeness.

我们在一门为期 16 周、包含 64 名设计新手的设计课程中对 Heritage Spark 进行了评估。参与者分别在使用和不使用工具包的条件下完成非遗数字化设计任务，并由五名设计专家对方案进行评价。结果表明，使用 Heritage Spark 完成的设计方案在新颖性、可行性与完整性方面均有显著提升。

Image Placeholder

Outcome / 研究结果

Heritage Spark supports novice designers as a cognitive scaffold, collaborative medium, and process guide. It enhances empathy, expands interdisciplinary knowledge, stimulates multi-dimensional inspiration, and fosters team synergy. More broadly, the project demonstrates how tangible, theory-driven tools can help novices navigate complex, knowledge-rich design domains.

Heritage Spark 可以同时发挥认知支架、协作媒介与流程向导的作用，帮助设计新手增强共情、拓展跨学科知识、激发多维创意，并促进团队协同。更进一步，该项目展示了如何通过理论驱动的实体工具，帮助设计新手应对复杂且知识密集的设计问题。
