Silkworm Box

Staff / 参与者

LiangHou

Design Awards / 获奖

· Silkworm Box, Red Dot Award, 2022

Silkworm Box is an intelligent sericulture kit designed for children’s nature education, combining traditional Chinese sericulture culture with smart environmental control. It regulates temperature, humidity, and lighting according to different stages of the silkworm life cycle, while a companion app enables real-time monitoring and provides educational guidance. Inspired by traditional cocooning frames, its lifting structure allows children to observe and participate in the complete silkworm life cycle, fostering curiosity, scientific thinking, hands-on learning, and parent–child engagement.

Silkworm Box 是一款面向儿童自然教育的智能养蚕套件，将中国传统养蚕文化与智能环境控制技术相结合。产品根据蚕从孵化、成长到结茧的不同阶段，自动调节适宜的温度、湿度与光照，并通过配套 App 实时监测养殖环境、提供养蚕知识。其升降结构灵感来源于传统方格簇，让儿童能够亲手观察并参与蚕的完整生命周期，在实践中培养自然探索、科学思维与动手能力，同时创造亲子共同参与的自然教育体验。
