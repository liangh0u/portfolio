手绘作品系列

Author / 作者

Liang Hou

手绘作品系列收录了我在求学期间完成的部分手绘练习与创作，主要包括以圆珠笔临摹和绘制的产品设计手稿，以及用于呈现产品概念的漫画式故事板。这些作品记录了我对产品形态、结构、透视与设计表达的持续练习。在手绘风格上，我受到产品设计师刘传凯的影响，并在学习与临摹其表现方式的过程中，逐渐探索适合自己的产品设计手绘语言。

Hand Sketching Collection brings together a selection of hand-drawn works created during my years of study. It mainly includes ballpoint-pen product sketches, along with comic-style storyboards illustrating product concepts, usage scenarios, and interactions. These works document my ongoing practice in product form, structure, perspective, and visual communication. My sketching style has been greatly influenced by product designer Liu Chuankai, whose expressive techniques I studied and referenced while gradually developing my own approach to product design sketching.
