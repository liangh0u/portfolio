NEXT Sealer

2024

LiangHou

NEXT Seal is an integrated hardware–software platform that combines traditional Chinese seal carving with AI generation and intelligent manufacturing. Through text input, AI-assisted seal character generation, and layout adjustment, users can create personalized seals without professional carving skills and turn digital designs into physical seals using an intelligent carving device. The project lowers the barrier to traditional seal creation and explores a more accessible and personalized way to engage with cultural heritage in the digital age.

NEXT Seal 是一套融合传统篆刻文化、AI 生成与智能制造的软硬件一体化创作平台。用户无需掌握专业篆书与篆刻技法，即可通过文字输入、篆字生成与版式调整完成个性化印章设计，并借助智能篆刻设备将数字设计转化为实体印章。项目以降低传统篆刻创作门槛为核心，探索传统文化在数字时代更易参与、更具个性化的创新体验。
