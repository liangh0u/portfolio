CoastalBam Jar

2024

LiangHou

CoastalBam Jar is a contemporary salt jar designed for Chinese coastal fishermen, combining traditional bamboo weaving with a durable metal liner to address the short lifespan and brine corrosion of conventional salt jars. The design preserves the traditional practice of collecting brine while drawing inspiration from Chinese bamboo weaving structures and regional folk culture. By bridging the natural warmth of bamboo with modern industrial manufacturing, it explores how traditional everyday objects can be reinterpreted and sustained in contemporary life.

CoastalBam Jar 是一款面向中国沿海渔民设计的现代盐罐，结合竹编工艺与金属内胆，回应传统盐罐使用寿命短、易受盐卤腐蚀等问题。设计延续渔民收集盐卤的传统使用习惯，并从中国传统竹编结构与地域民俗中提取设计语言，在天然竹材的温润质感与现代工业制造之间建立联系，探索传统生活器物在当代语境下的创新与延续。
