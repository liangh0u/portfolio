CoastalBam Jar

2024

LiangHou

We present CoastalBam Jar, a lightweight coastal serving vessel that combines a raised base with a removable woven strainer. The design brings a practical serving ritual and natural material expression to the table.

我们提出了 CoastalBam Jar，一款轻巧的海岸主题餐桌器皿，将抬高的底座与可拆卸的编织滤网结合。设计以自然材质和实用的取用方式丰富餐桌体验。
