PoemCraft

December 2025  2025 年 12 月

Liang Hou, Youwei Feng, Haoxuan Liu

We present PoemCraft, a constructive XR learning system designed to support the understanding of poetic imagery. By integrating constructivist learning theory with immersive interaction, PoemCraft shifts learners from “viewing poetic scenes” to “actively constructing poetic scenes.” Addressing the highly abstract and individually constructed nature of poetic imagery, PoemCraft enables learners to select, arrange, and construct poetic imagery through natural hand gestures. It further transforms tabletop-scale MR scenes into immersive VR environments, allowing learners to step into the poetic worlds they have created to experience, compare, and reflect on their interpretations.

我们提出了 PoemCraft，一套面向诗词意境理解的建构式 XR 学习系统，通过将建构主义学习理论与沉浸式交互结合，引导学习者从“观看诗境”转向“主动创造诗境”。针对诗词意境具有高度抽象性与个体建构性的特点，PoemCraft 允许学习者通过自然手势选择、组织和搭建诗词中的意象，并将桌面尺度的 MR 场景进一步转换为可沉浸漫游的 VR 空间，使学习者能够进入自己创造的诗境进行体验、比较与反思。

Background / 背景

Poetic imagery is abstract and highly personal. While XR can transform text into immersive spatial experiences, most existing approaches present learners with predefined scenes, leaving little room for individual interpretation.

诗词意境具有高度的抽象性与个体建构性。尽管 XR 可以将文字转化为空间体验，但现有方式大多向学习者呈现预设场景，难以支持学习者形成和表达自己的理解。

Image Placeholder

Research Question / 研究问题

What if learners could build a poetic world instead of simply viewing one? We explore how creating, experiencing, and reflecting in XR can support learners in constructing their own understanding of poetry.

如果学习者不只是观看诗境，而是亲手创造它，会发生什么？我们探索如何通过 XR 中的创造、体验与反思，帮助学习者主动建构自己的诗词理解。

Image Placeholder

Design Framework / 设计框架

We translated constructivist learning into a five-stage XR experience: Scaffolding → Construction → Exploration → Collaboration → Reflection. Together, these stages turn spatial creation into a continuous process of meaning-making.

我们将建构主义学习过程转化为五个连续的 XR 体验阶段：科普 → 搭建 → 漫游 → 协作 → 反思，使空间创造成为学习者持续理解、表达与修正意义的过程。

Image Placeholder

Design / 设计

PoemCraft enables learners to learn, build, explore, collaborate, and reflect. Learners construct poetic scenes with natural gestures, step into their creations, compare different interpretations, and continuously refine their understanding.

PoemCraft 支持学习者进行理解、搭建、漫游、协作与反思。学习者通过自然手势创造诗境，进入自己的作品进行沉浸体验，并通过比较与反思不断修正自己的理解。

Image Placeholder

XR Interaction / XR 交互

PoemCraft connects two spatial scales: learners first construct a miniature poetic world in MR, then enter it at full scale in VR. This transition connects spatial expression with embodied experience.

PoemCraft 连接了两种空间尺度：学习者首先在 MR 中以上帝视角搭建微缩诗境，再进入 VR 中以第一人称沉浸体验，让空间表达与身体体验形成连接。

Image Placeholder

Evaluation / 设计评估

A controlled study with 20 middle school students compared Constructive XR with Presentation-based XR. Constructive XR significantly improved semantic and poetic-scene understanding, while also achieving higher learning motivation, presence, and usability.

我们邀请 20 名中学生参与对照实验，对比建构式 XR 与呈现式 XR。结果表明，建构式 XR 显著提升了学习者的语义理解与诗境理解，并在学习动机、沉浸感与系统可用性方面表现更优。

Image Placeholder

Outcome / 研究结果

PoemCraft suggests that XR can do more than visualize knowledge—it can provide a space for learners to construct meaning through creation. Instead of presenting a single interpretation, it gives learners the tools to form their own.

PoemCraft 表明，XR 不仅可以用于知识的可视化，也可以成为学习者通过创造建构意义的空间。它不再呈现一个唯一的解释，而是为学习者提供形成自己理解的工具。
