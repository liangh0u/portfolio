PoemCraft

Staff / 参与者

Liang Hou, Youwei Feng, Haoxuan Liu

Design Awards / 获奖

· PoemCraft, 中国移动应用创新赛三等奖, 2025

We present PoemCraft, a constructive XR learning system designed to support the understanding of poetic imagery. By integrating constructivist learning theory with immersive interaction, PoemCraft shifts learners from “viewing poetic scenes” to “actively constructing poetic scenes.” Addressing the highly abstract and individually constructed nature of poetic imagery, PoemCraft enables learners to select, arrange, and construct poetic imagery through natural hand gestures. It further transforms tabletop-scale MR scenes into immersive VR environments, allowing learners to step into the poetic worlds they have created to experience, compare, and reflect on their interpretations.

我们提出了 PoemCraft，一套面向诗词意境理解的建构式 XR 学习系统，通过将建构主义学习理论与沉浸式交互结合，引导学习者从“观看诗境”转向“主动创造诗境”。针对诗词意境具有高度抽象性与个体建构性的特点，PoemCraft 允许学习者通过自然手势选择、组织和搭建诗词中的意象，并将桌面尺度的 MR 场景进一步转换为可沉浸漫游的 VR 空间，使学习者能够进入自己创造的诗境进行体验、比较与反思。

Background & Research Question / 背景与研究问题

Poetic imagery is abstract, personal, and shaped by each learner's own interpretation. Although XR can transform poetry into immersive spatial experiences, most existing approaches present predefined scenes, leaving learners with little room to construct and express their own understanding. This led us to ask: what if learners could build a poetic world instead of simply viewing one? We explore how creating, experiencing, and reflecting in XR can support learners in actively constructing their understanding of poetry.

诗词意境具有高度的抽象性与个体建构性。尽管 XR 可以将诗词转化为空间体验，但现有方式大多向学习者呈现预设场景，难以支持他们形成和表达自己的理解。因此，我们提出研究问题：如果学习者不只是观看诗境，而是亲手创造它，会发生什么？我们探索如何通过 XR 中的创造、体验与反思，帮助学习者主动建构自己的诗词理解。

Design / 设计

We present PoemCraft, a poetic-imagery experience tool grounded in constructivist learning. Learners create poetic scenes through natural gestures, enter their own creations for immersive exploration, and continuously refine their understanding through comparison and reflection.

我们提出了 PoemCraft，一种结合建构主义学习的诗词意境体验工具。学习者通过自然手势创造诗境，进入自己的作品进行沉浸体验，并通过比较与反思不断修正自己的理解。

XR Interaction / XR 交互

PoemCraft connects two spatial scales: learners first select a terrain and construct a miniature poetic world in MR, then enter it at full scale in VR. This transition connects spatial expression with embodied experience.

PoemCraft 连接了两种空间尺度：学习者首先在 MR 中选择地形，再以上帝视角自主搭建微缩诗境，随后进入 VR 中以第一人称沉浸游览，让空间表达与身体体验形成连接。

Gesture Interaction / 手势交互

To support learners as they construct, adjust, and experience poetic scenes in the virtual environment, the system defines a consistent set of gesture-based interaction rules. All gestures are organized into four categories: summon, pinch, confirm, and exit.

为了保证学习者在虚拟环境中的建构、调整与体验过程，系统为产品定义了一套手势交互规则，所有手势被归纳为四类：召唤类、捏合类、确认类与退出类。

Movement / 移动交互

Learners can explore through natural walking or use gaze-and-gesture teleportation to reach distant areas safely.

学习者既可通过真实行走自然探索，也可结合注视与手势进行传送，在有限空间内安全到达远处场景。

In-scene Interaction / 意境内交互

Learners can shift the time of day and weather, observing how light, color, atmosphere, and sound reshape the poetic scene.

学习者可以切换时段与天气，观察光照、色彩、氛围和声音如何共同改变诗词意境。

Evaluation / 设计评估

Experiment Design / 实验设计

We conducted a counterbalanced within-subjects study with 20 middle school students. Participants were divided into four groups and completed two poetry-learning tasks using both PoemCraft's Constructive XR and Presentation-based XR in different orders. Each task included tool use, a learning assessment, and a questionnaire, followed by semi-structured interviews.

我们采用平衡顺序的被试内实验设计，邀请 20 名中学生参与研究。参与者被分为四组，以不同顺序使用 PoemCraft 建构式 XR 与呈现式 XR 完成两项古诗词学习任务；每项任务均包含工具使用、学习测试与问卷填写，实验结束后进行半结构化访谈。

Experiment Results / 实验结果

The results show that Constructive XR significantly improved learners’ semantic understanding and comprehension of poetic imagery, while also achieving better outcomes in learning motivation, presence, and system usability. PoemCraft moves beyond presenting a single interpretation of poetic imagery and instead provides learners with tools to form their own understanding.

结果表明，建构式 XR 显著提升了学习者的语义理解与诗境理解，并在学习动机、沉浸感与系统可用性方面表现更优。PoemCraft 让诗词意境不再呈现一个唯一的解释，而是为学习者提供形成自己理解的工具。
