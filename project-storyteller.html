Storyteller

2025

LiangHou

We present Storyteller, a tactile listening companion designed to create a warmer and more intuitive storytelling experience. Its physical form invites people to interact with stories through touch as well as sound.

我们提出了 Storyteller，一款以触觉交互为核心的聆听伴侣，带来更温暖、直觉的故事体验。它让人们在声音之外，也能通过触摸与故事建立联系。
